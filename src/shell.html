How to fly

No controller found. Press any controller button to connect it.

Fly a lap

  1. Press A or Enter to lift off.
  2. Push the right stick (or ↑) forward to accelerate. Let go to hold the speed, pull back to brake. RB or Space is boost.
  3. Fly between the checkered spires to start the clock.
  4. Bank to follow the chevrons. Altitude holds itself.
  5. Round all seven spires, then cross the gate again.

Left thumb holds Boost. Left hand: stick yaw and climb, trigger boost, grip land, X altitude hold, Y menu, stick click fold. Right hand: stick bank, and forward and back for speed (centred holds it), trigger boost, grip hand over to SI, A take off (or Fly it when watching SI), B next view, stick click path. In the menu point with the right hand and pull the trigger: Fly it or Watch SI, every view, back to pad, fold, max detail, twilight, sound and Exit VR. Ship view: A jumps to the nearest deck, right stick zooms, stick click range finder. Pool deck: A swaps ships. On the ship (Ship view or Pool deck) while SI flies, X starts the Falcon 9 T-minus countdown; it is also in the menu.

Read the course

Red spire: turn left
Green spire: turn right
Checkered: start and finish
Green chevrons: speed up
Yellow chevrons: hold speed
Red chevrons: slow down
Hit a spire +3 s. Wrong side +5 s. Hit the water and the lap is void.
